Sporting Regulations

1. Parc Fermé (The Deadline)

  • Strategies are strictly locked at the start of Qualifying. You can change your team any number of times before this.
  • Incomplete submissions will be rejected. If you fail to submit a team, your score will be 5 points lower than the lowest scoring player that week.

2. Driver Scoring (P1, P2, P3, P10, P11, P21, P22)

  • Exact Match Bonus: +2 Points.
  • Differential Penalty: -1 Point for every position your prediction misses by. Target is to minimize negative points!
  • All DNFs (or pre-announced non-participating drivers) will automatically be scored as finishing in P22.

3. Constructor Scoring (1st, 2nd, 5th, 6th, 11th)

  • Constructors are ranked based on the sum of both their cars' race positions (lower sum = better rank).
  • The Span Rule (Ties): If teams tie for a rank (e.g., tie for 4th), they occupy ranks 4 AND 5. Predicting either team in 4th OR 5th awards full points!

4. Strategic Wildcards

  • Race Biggest Loser: +5 Points for correctly predicting the driver whose rank falls the most from their starting grid position. Ties award points to all involved!
  • Sprint Weekends: +5 Points each for predicting the Sprint's Biggest Gainer and Biggest Loser.

5. Championship Prize

  • The overall tournament winner will receive ₹500 from each of the other players.
  • Season-Long Predictions: Predict the World Drivers' Champion (+50 pts) and World Constructors' Champion (+25 pts). This form permanently locks at the start of Australian GP Qualifying!
