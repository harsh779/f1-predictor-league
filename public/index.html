Sporting Regulations & Scoring

Everything you need to know about how the F1 2026 Strategy League works.

Locks 1 minute before Qualifying

All picks are final once the lock begins.

Overview

Submit driver, constructor, wildcard, and season-long picks before lockout. The app keeps existing backend scoring and lock behavior unchanged.

Parc Ferme

Strategies are strictly locked 1 minute before Qualifying. Incomplete submissions are rejected before submit; missed rounds receive the existing lowest-score-minus-5 penalty.

Driver Scoring

Driver predictions include P1, P2, P3, P10, P11, P21, and P22. Exact Match Bonus is +2 points. Differential Penalty is -1 point per position missed. DNFs or non-participating drivers count as P22.

Constructor Scoring

Constructor predictions include C1, C2, C5, C6, and C11. Constructor ranking is based on combined finishing positions. The Span Rule applies to constructor rank ties.

Strategic Wildcards

Current wildcard logic remains unchanged: Race Biggest Loser, Sprint Biggest Gainer, and Sprint Biggest Loser when sprint fields are enabled.

Penalty System

Missed Round = lowest score of the round minus 5. New Joiner = one-time lowest total score in standings minus 5.

Championship Prize

The overall tournament winner currently receives Rs 500 from each other player. Season-long picks award WDC +50 and WCC +25, locked at Australian GP Qualifying.
